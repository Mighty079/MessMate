# MessMate 🍽️

**MessMate** is an Android application designed for college hostel students to track their mess food menus, stay on top of meal timings with offline reminder notifications, and manage daily routines.

## ✨ Features

- **📸 AI Menu Extraction**: Upload a photo of your hostel mess menu and let Gemini AI automatically digitize all meal timings and food items for the entire week.
- **🔔 Reliable Meal Notifications**: Automated reminder alarms (at meal time or 5/10/15 minutes before) that wake up during Doze mode and self-renew weekly.
- **🍛 Next Meal & Today's Menu**: Dynamic home dashboard displaying upcoming meals, current timings, and day-to-day items at a glance.
- **📅 Weekly Menu Browser**: Seamlessly browse breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner schedules across Monday through Sunday.
- **⏰ Daily Routine Tracker**: Manage tasks, study hours, and classes with offline alarms.
- **🎨 Modern Material 3 UI**: Clean Compose interface with custom themes and dark mode support.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Kotlin** & **Jetpack Compose**
- **Material 3** Design System
- **Google Gemini API** (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite`) for Menu OCR
- **Android AlarmManager** with Exact & AlarmClock scheduling
- **Kotlinx Serialization** & Local Storage
